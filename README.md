# Payvibes
🚀 Overview
The Payvibes mangement System an advanced, tech-driven platform designed to automate and optimize the entire supply chain and retail distribution network. By replacing traditional, manual record-keeping with real-time tracking and intelligent automation, SDMS bridges the gap between warehouses, delivery agents, and retail shops—ensuring products reach the right shelves at the exact right time with zero blind spots.

✨ Key Features
1. Live Inventory & Warehouse Control
Real-Time Stock Monitoring: Instantly track stock levels across multiple warehouses, godowns, and distribution points from a single dashboard.

Batch & Expiry Management: Accurately track products by batch numbers and expiry dates to eliminate dead stock and reduce financial losses.

Smart Replenishment Alerts: Automated low-stock alerts and threshold notifications prevent unexpected stockouts in fast-moving outlets.

2. Order-to-Delivery & Route Optimization
Seamless Order Processing: Convert sales orders into digital invoices instantly with built-in stock validation and automated approvals.

Optimized Route Planning: Integrated delivery tracking and route mapping help field teams cover retail locations faster while saving fuel and time.

Digital Proof of Delivery: Record precise logs of what was delivered to each shop to maintain complete delivery transparency.

3. Customer & Financial Ledger Management
Multi-Shop Management: Easily handle dozens or hundreds of retail customers under one unified system.

Automated Payment Tracking: Keep a clear, running ledger for every customer so pending balances, credits, and recoveries are never lost.

Instant Notifications: Automated payment reminders and order confirmations sent directly via SMS or WhatsApp.

4. Advanced Analytics & Reporting
Profitability Insights: Quickly identify which retail routes, products, or customers are driving the highest revenue.

Comprehensive Dashboards: Access 50+ customizable reports covering sales velocity, cash flow, outstanding debts, and staff performance.

💻 Tech Stack & Architecture
Frontend: Responsive web interface and mobile-friendly layouts for field agents.

Backend: Secure, scalable cloud architecture ensuring high availability and fast query processing.

Database: Centralized cloud database with offline sync capabilities for uninterrupted field operations.

🎯 Target Audience
FMCG & Consumer Goods Distributors looking to digitize middle-mile logistics.

Wholesale Suppliers managing extensive networks of small retail shops.

Logistics & Delivery Operations requiring precise tracking of orders, payments, and inventory.
